Indic Keyboard is the first Android keyboard for Indian languages, now rebuilt from the ground up. Type in your language anywhere you would type in English: messages, email, search and more.

- 31 languages, 96 layouts, 21 scripts
- Gesture typing, even on transliteration layouts - a first for any keyboard
- Companion typing: type your language from the English keyboard, no switching
- Predictive transliteration: type "namaste", get नमस्ते
- Next-word prediction in 20 languages, spell check
- Clipboard history and password manager autofill
- Physical keyboard support
- Material You themes, emoji search
- Free and open source. No ads, no tracking.

# How do I enable it?
A setup wizard walks you through turning on the keyboard and picking your languages and layouts.

# I see a warning about "collecting data" when enabling the keyboard
Android shows this for every third party keyboard. Indic Keyboard does not collect anything you type.

# What is a keyboard layout?
Each language offers different ways to type:
- Transliteration: type in English letters and they turn into your script. "namaste" becomes नमस्ते.
- Predictive Transliteration: transliteration that learns your words and suggests the right spelling.
- Inscript: the Government of India standard layout. If you know it on desktop, it works here too.
- Phonetic: letters sit where their English sound-alikes are.
- Compact: no shift key. Long press a letter for more options.
